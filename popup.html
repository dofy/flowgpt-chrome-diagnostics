How to run a test

  1. Open the FlowGPT or Emochi page where you saw the error.
  2. Click Start recording and repeat what failed in the same tab.
  3. Open this extension again. Download the ZIP or copy the report, then send it to support.

Recording saves failed request URLs and diagnostic details on this device. The report can include your public IP. You choose whether to share the report with support.
